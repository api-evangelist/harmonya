# Harmonya

> Harmonya is a product intelligence company for consumer packaged goods (CPG) manufacturers and
> retailers, founded in 2021 with offices in New York City and Tel Aviv. It reads live product
> listings, pack and label copy, manufacturer specifications, syndicated feeds and consumer
> reviews, and turns them into normalized product attributes at the UPC level, plus demand themes
> and consumer-voice signals layered on top.

## Developer surface — what an agent should know first

Harmonya publishes **no public API**. As of 2026-08-22:

- There is no `api.`, `docs.`, `developer.` or `developers.harmonya.com` — all four are NXDOMAIN.
- `www.harmonya.com` returns 404 for `/openapi.json`, `/openapi.yaml`, `/swagger.json`,
  `/api-docs`, `/docs`, `/redoc`, `/graphql` and every `/.well-known/` path.
- The 150-URL sitemap at `https://www.harmonya.com/sitemap.xml` contains no developer,
  documentation, API, pricing or status page.
- No first-party SDK exists on npm, PyPI or RubyGems; no public GitHub organization is claimed.
- No MCP server, no A2A agent card, no AsyncAPI, no webhook catalog, no Postman workspace.

The only programmatic surface is the tenant application backend at `https://app.harmonya.com`,
reached by the Harmonya web app and protected by the company's Auth0 tenant
(`https://harmonya.us.auth0.com`, audience `https://app.harmonya.com`). It is undocumented and
requires a customer account. Access begins at https://www.harmonya.com/get-a-demo.

## Products

- **Demand Intelligence** — https://www.harmonya.com/solutions/demand-intelligence
  Clusters enriched product attributes into demand themes, theme maturity curves and brand
  scorecards for a category.
- **Consumer Intelligence** — https://www.harmonya.com/solutions/consumer-intelligence
  Consumer review language connected to the themes shaping demand, for messaging and innovation.
- **Attribute Intelligence** — https://www.harmonya.com/solutions/attribute-intelligence
  Market and measured attributes enriched at the UPC level: claims, ingredients, flavors, pack,
  dietary positioning, and measured values such as grams of protein.
- **Nutrition Profile** — https://www.harmonya.com/solutions/nutrition-profile
  Measured grams of protein, sugar and fiber per product.
- **Product Catalog** — https://www.harmonya.com/solutions/product-catalog
  A maintained product layer across a customer's schemas and taxonomies, offered as a Standard
  catalog or a Custom catalog maintained to the customer's spec, delivered as a feed into a PIM,
  data lake, dashboards and AI systems.
